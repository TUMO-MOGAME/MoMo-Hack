# A3 overlay — MoMo Kasi

Appended to the shared A3 brief.

## The actual user

Grade against this device and context, not a desktop with a mouse:

- **Low-end Android**, 320px wide, cracked screen, **direct Highveld sunlight**.
- **Slow 3G**, or no connection at all — ICASA out-of-bundle disconnection is abrupt and
  mid-session (`docs/00` §6a).
- Often **one-handed**, sometimes with gloves, at a noisy taxi rank.
- English is frequently a second or third language.

Contrast below 4.5:1 is not a technicality here — it means a person cannot read their balance
outside. Treat it as **High**, not Medium.

## Money-specific accessibility

- **Amounts must use tabular numerals.** A balance whose digits shift width as it updates reads as
  untrustworthy. The `.tabular` utility exists for this.
- **The confirmation card is the highest-stakes surface in the app.** Confirm and Cancel must be
  visually distinct, far apart, and both reachable by keyboard with a visible focus ring. Two grey
  buttons side by side is **High**.
- Amounts must be announced correctly by a screen reader — "twelve rand fifty", not "R one two
  five zero".
- Status colour (success green, pending amber) must never be the **only** signal. Check for a text
  or icon companion.

## Voice and accessibility are the same mechanism (docs/13 §7)

An artifact's spoken summary **is** its `aria-label`. Verify they match. If a screen-reader user and
a voice user get different sentences, that is a finding — the design intent is one string.

Voice is never the only route to anything. Every voice affordance needs a text equivalent, and the
"type instead" control must always be reachable.

## Artifact panel and bottom sheet

- The mobile sheet is a modal dialog: check `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, focus trap, focus
  restoration on close, and Escape to dismiss.
- A `confirm` artifact must open **full height**. A partly off-screen payment confirmation is
  **High**.
- The sheet must be dismissible by keyboard, not only by swipe.
- Touch targets ≥ 44px.

## Motion

`prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured in `globals.css`. Verify it actually suppresses the sheet
animation and the thinking dots, not just declares the media query.

## Not in scope

- Native mobile accessibility — there is no native app (`docs/00` §7).
- Cross-browser AT testing beyond Chromium — a stated limitation in `docs/04` §13.
